Last updated: November 21, 2026

1Introduction

Welcome to Al Afreet ("the App", "we", "our", or "us").

This Privacy Policy explains how Al Afreet accesses, collects, uses, processes, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

We are committed to respecting your privacy and handling your information responsibly. By using the application, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

Users may optionally sign in using their Google accounts through Supabase Authentication.

When you choose to sign in, we may process:

  • Email address.
  • Unique account identifier.
  • Name and profile picture, if provided by your Google account.

This information is used for authentication and account management. Signing in is not required to access features that do not need an account.

2.2 Contacts Information

Al Afreet may request access to your device contacts to provide contact-related functionality and allow you to select people for emergency assistance.

  • Contact information may include names and phone numbers.
  • Contacts are accessed when you use a feature that requires them.
  • Contacts used for local functionality are processed on your device.
  • We do not sell contact information.
  • If you choose to send an emergency message, selected contact details may be used to address that message through the relevant messaging or communication application.

2.3 Camera Information

The application may request camera access to scan QR codes and barcodes.

Camera access is activated when you use the scanning feature. Images captured for scanning are processed for that purpose and are not retained by us through this feature.

2.4 Location Information

Al Afreet may access your approximate or precise device location when you use features that require location information.

Location-based functionality may include:

  • Determining your location for Qibla direction.
  • Providing location-based prayer-related information.
  • Emergency assistance when you explicitly activate the emergency feature.

Location handling:

  • Location access is requested when a relevant feature needs it.
  • Location may be obtained using approximate or precise location permissions, depending on the feature and your device settings.
  • Location is not collected for advertising purposes.
  • When you activate emergency assistance, your current location may be included in an emergency message and shared with the contacts you select.
  • Emergency location sharing is initiated by your action.
  • You can revoke location permission through your Android device settings.

Al Afreet does not require continuous background location access for the features described in this policy. Location access may be unavailable if you deny or revoke the relevant permission.

2.5 Device and Technical Information

The application may process basic device information, application settings, diagnostic information, and locally stored preferences necessary to operate its features and maintain application stability.

3How We Use Information

Information accessed or collected through Al Afreet may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain application functionality.
  • Authenticate users who choose to sign in.
  • Manage user accounts.
  • Provide contact-related functionality.
  • Scan and process QR codes and barcodes.
  • Calculate Qibla direction and provide location-based prayer features.
  • Support emergency assistance when explicitly activated.
  • Improve application performance, reliability, and security.
  • Prevent misuse and unauthorized access.

6Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Location information may be processed on your device to provide location-based features. When you activate emergency assistance, location information may be included in a message shared with your selected recipients.

No method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
