1Introduction

Welcome to Al Afreet ("the App", "we", "our", or "us").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Al Afreet mobile application.

By using our application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

Al Afreet allows users to optionally sign in using their Google accounts through Supabase Authentication.

When you choose to sign in, we may collect and process:

  • Email address.
  • Unique account identifier.
  • Basic profile information, such as your name and profile picture, when provided.

This information is used to authenticate users and provide account-related services.

You can use the application's available features without signing in.

2.2 Contacts Information

The application may request access to your device contacts to provide contact-related features.

  • Contacts are accessed and processed locally on your device.
  • Contact names and phone numbers are not uploaded to our servers.
  • We do not share, sell, or disclose your contacts to third parties.

2.3 Camera Information

The application may request camera access to scan QR codes and barcodes.

Camera access is used only when you activate the scanning functionality. Camera images are not collected or uploaded to our servers through this feature.

2.4 Device Information

The application may process basic technical information and locally stored preferences necessary to operate its features and improve your experience.

3How We Use Information

We use collected or processed information to:

  • Provide and maintain application functionality.
  • Authenticate users who choose to sign in.
  • Manage user accounts.
  • Provide QR code and barcode generation and scanning features.
  • Improve application performance and stability.
  • Protect the application against unauthorized access and misuse.
